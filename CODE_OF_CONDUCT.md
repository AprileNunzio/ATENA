# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

*Versione italiana in fondo al documento.*

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy
community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
* Reviewing code and design decisions on their technical merits, and explaining the reasoning behind a request

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
* Publishing recordings, camera frames, voice samples or any other personal data collected by ad Atena
  installation about people who have not consented to it
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will
take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki
edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate
reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially
representing the community in public spaces. Community spaces include the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, review comments, and any direct communication about the Atena project. Examples of representing
our community include using an official e-mail address, posting via an official social media account, or acting
as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders
responsible for enforcement at **aprilenunzio88@gmail.com** (subject: *Code of Conduct*). All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and acknowledged within **three working days**.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident. A
report is shared only with the people who need it to act on it, and the identity of the reporter is not
disclosed without their consent. If the report concerns a community leader, it can be sent to any other
maintainer so that the person concerned does not take part in the decision.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action
they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the
community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the
violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved,
including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time.
This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media.
Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a
specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited
interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may
lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained
inappropriate behavior, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of
individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

# Codice di condotta (italiano)

Questo progetto adotta il **Contributor Covenant 2.1**. In sintesi:

- **Impegno**: partecipare al progetto deve essere un'esperienza priva di molestie per chiunque, senza
  distinzioni di età, corporatura, disabilità, etnia, genere, esperienza, istruzione, condizione sociale,
  nazionalità, aspetto, religione o orientamento.
- **Comportamenti positivi**: empatia e gentilezza, rispetto delle opinioni diverse, critiche costruttive date e
  ricevute con garbo, assunzione di responsabilità, attenzione al bene della comunità, revisioni del codice
  basate sul merito tecnico e con le ragioni di ogni richiesta spiegate.
- **Comportamenti inaccettabili**: linguaggio o immagini sessualizzati, insulti e attacchi personali o
  politici, molestie pubbliche o private, pubblicazione di dati privati altrui senza permesso, **pubblicazione di
  registrazioni, immagini della webcam, campioni vocali o altri dati personali raccolti da un'installazione di
  Atena su persone che non hanno acconsentito**, ogni altra condotta non appropriata in un contesto professionale.
- **Ambito**: repository, issue, pull request, discussioni, commenti di revisione e ogni comunicazione sul
  progetto; vale anche quando si rappresenta ufficialmente la comunità.
- **Segnalazioni**: a **aprilenunzio88@gmail.com** (oggetto «Codice di condotta»). Conferma di ricezione entro
  **tre giorni lavorativi**; riservatezza garantita a chi segnala; se la segnalazione riguarda un responsabile,
  può essere inviata a un altro maintainer.
- **Conseguenze** (in ordine di gravità): correzione (avviso privato), avvertimento, sospensione temporanea,
  esclusione permanente.
